Startup Speeder Upper

Takes about 9 seconds off your Brick's startup. One button to speed up, one button to undo.

One moment...

You need

  • A Brick, Brick Hammer or Brick Pro on TrimUI firmware 1.1.1. Anything else gets a polite no.
  • Chrome or Edge, on a computer or an Android phone.
  • A USB cable that carries data. Switch the Brick on before you plug it in.

It doesn't matter what's on the SD card, or whether there is one. Running NextUI? There's also a pak.

What the Brick stops doing

  • Waiting for a computer's flashing tool that isn't there.
  • Printing boot messages to a serial port nobody's listening to.
  • Napping for up to 250 ms at a time while switching on the screen.
  • Setting up 16 RAM disks. It gets one.
  • Loading 21 MB of GPU driver debug data.
  • Reading TrimUI's 2 MB menu program just to learn its own model name.
  • Waiting for a hotplug event before mounting the SD card.

If it won't connect

  • The Brick isn't in the list? Try another cable. Many only charge.
  • You use adb on this computer? Run adb kill-server first. It holds on to the Brick.
  • On Windows the Brick may need a WinUSB driver first. Zadig installs one.
