# Third-party files in this pak

- `bin/minui-presenter`: minui-presenter 0.14.0 by Jose Diaz-Gonzalez,
  MIT License, https://github.com/josegonzalez/minui-presenter. The release
  build `minui-presenter-tg5040-nextui`, unchanged.
- `data/*/pvrsrvkm.ko`: the PowerVR GPU driver from TrimUI's v1.1.1 firmware
  for each model (Imagination Technologies, dual MIT/GPLv2), with its debug
  sections removed. Its code, data and symbols are unchanged.
